Our Purpose

Why CookSmart Exists

Every day, people stare into their fridge wondering what to make. CookSmart removes that friction by letting you search recipes based on what you actually have, how much time you've got, and how confident you're feeling in the kitchen.

Our Objectives

What We Set Out to Build

A responsive, single-page recipe portal with a genuine design system — no boilerplate templates — covering discovery, planning, favourites and a transparent AI-assisted suggestion feature, all backed by pre-populated data.

Mission & Vision

What Drives Us

Mission

To make everyday cooking decisions effortless by turning whatever is in your kitchen into a complete, confident recipe.

Vision

A world where nobody wastes ingredients or time deciding what to eat — smart suggestions, always a tap away.
